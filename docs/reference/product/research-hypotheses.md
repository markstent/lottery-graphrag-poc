# Research Hypotheses

## H1

Graph-enhanced retrieval improves evidence recall as relationship depth increases.

## H2

Ontology-enhanced retrieval improves cross-market concept matching beyond graph structure alone.

## H3

Simple factual questions show little or no advantage from graph augmentation.

## H4

Graph augmentation improves provenance and evidence-path explainability.

## H5

Graph augmentation improves discovery of planted multi-entity patterns.

## H6

Graph augmentation may increase retrieval latency but can improve answer quality and context efficiency.

## H7

Richer structured context may allow a smaller local model to achieve quality closer to a larger model.

All hypotheses must be measured rather than assumed true.
